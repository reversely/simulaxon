# Simulaxon

## Commits

- Commit and push to `main` as soon as a change is ready, so the team sees it.
- Subject in Conventional Commits form: `type(scope): subject`, with type `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `refactor`, `chore`, `build` or `test`.
- Keep the subject brief: imperative, lowercase after the colon, no period, 70 characters or fewer. Add a body only when the reason is not obvious from the subject.
- One logical change per commit.
- No `Co-Authored-By` or other assistant attribution lines.
